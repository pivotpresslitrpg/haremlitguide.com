# Harem Lit Guide editorial campaign

Research date: September 15, 2026. Destination: Harem Lit Guide only. No changes to Harem-Lit.com, LitRPG Critic, or Fantasy Ranked.

## Coverage and voices

Eleven scoped manuscript-based editorial assessments, not fabricated consumer testimonials or full retail-edition read-throughs. Cole (fictional male) covers Isekai Emperor 1–3, Rhea (fictional female) covers Dungeon Champions 1–3, Grant (fictional male) covers Fate’s Enforcer 1–3, and Tessa (fictional female) covers Isle of the Amazonian Elves 1–2. Gender is a disclosed persona attribute, not evidence of a real reviewer’s identity or lived experience.

Cole emphasizes bawdy civic competence; Rhea emphasizes party dynamics and emotional agency; Grant emphasizes duty, dialogue, and supernatural intrigue; Tessa emphasizes warrior competence, community, and family-building consequences. Adult themes are discussed critically without graphic scene recreation.

Each manifest binds the inspected manuscript using SHA-256 and lists the actual reading scope. This pass examined introductory material and selected scene passages, not every chapter. Private manuscripts are not uploaded or committed. Online sources are linked with limits: author promotion is not reader feedback, first-book and series-level comments are not attributed to sequels, indexed excerpts are identified when direct retrieval was blocked, and isolated opinions are not presented as consensus. No invented ratings, sales claims, personal experiences, or reader quotations are included.

Dungeon Champions 4 (approximately 22,000 words) and Amazonian Elves 3 (partial combined chapters) were excluded: these are not established finished books for this campaign. Existing public reviews remain unchanged; these essays add specific critical angles rather than masquerading as new independent consumer reviews.

## Marketing intel brief

Domain: organic book discovery and review quality. Google’s [review guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews), updated December 10, 2025 and checked September 15, 2026, favors original, useful analysis and benefits/drawbacks over prescribed length. Confidence: high for the published guidance, no inference of guaranteed rankings.

Application: exact series/book/author identification, distinctive titles, natural genre terms, substantive reader-fit caveats, and visible source limits. No unsupported search-volume estimates or promise to maximize SEO. The essays differentiate between crunchy harem LitRPG, GameLit urban fantasy, and lighter progression fantasy instead of attaching all keywords to every book.

Open evidence limitations: no complete retail-edition or audiobook assessment; several sequel-specific feedback gaps; no independent blind editorial reviewer; future scheduled execution and search performance not yet observed. Small-print disclosure must remain readable, using the existing text-sm class, and publisher ownership is stated explicitly.

## Verification contract

Root: the eleven approved-scope articles are queued on Harem Lit Guide at weekly per-series intervals, with stable disclosed identities and no early public rendering. Evidence required: source/manifest validation, ordered repeat-safe publisher rehearsal in a temporary directory, production build, future-date isolated preview, visible disclosure, exact remote queue readback and deployment identity. No schema, endpoint, commerce, auth, or secret changes.

All-green counterexample to test: a successful source commit could still fail to publish because Actions bot pushes do not trigger deployment. The workflow completion trigger addresses ordering. A build without the future drafts cannot prove their rendering; an isolated future-date build is required. Automated checks cannot prove that an entire retail book was read; the articles explicitly limit that claim.

Off-claim finding: the existing generic book-report pipeline can still select these series independently. This campaign does not rewrite historical articles or redesign that pipeline. Its queue exhausts after the listed books; it is not an endless review generator.
