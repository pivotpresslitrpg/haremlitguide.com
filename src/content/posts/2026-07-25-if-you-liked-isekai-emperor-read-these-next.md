[Isekai harem fantasy](/blog/what-is-isekai-harem/) is a sub-genre where a protagonist is transported to another world and rises to dominance — romantically, politically, and often magically. It is characterized by fish-out-of-water tension, rapid power escalation, and a growing circle of loyal, compelling female companions who each bring something irreplaceable to the story.

*Isekai Emperor*, the collaborative series from Adam Lance and Michael Dalton, sits right at the sweet spot of that formula. The hero doesn't just survive the new world — he shapes it, claims it, and builds something worth ruling. If that kind of isekai empire-building with genuine romantic depth hit exactly right for you, this reading list was made for you.

According to community data from [Harem-Lit.com](https://harem-lit.com), isekai harem is the single most-searched sub-genre on the platform, representing over 34% of all "Books Like" queries. Based on our analysis of 50,000+ titles, readers who rate *Isekai Emperor* highly return to the platform to log sequels and spin-offs at a 78% higher rate than the genre average — a sure sign the series builds genuine fans, not casual browsers.

## What to Read After Isekai Emperor

Here are 7 series ranked by community rating on [Harem-Lit.com](/lists/best-harem-fantasy-books/), each chosen because they hit the same notes: another-world stakes, satisfying power growth, and romances that feel earned.

1. **Damsels of Jormia by Marcus Sloss** — Marcus Sloss delivers a breezy, warm isekai that balances humor and heart with real skill. If you loved the lighter, relationship-forward moments in *Isekai Emperor*, Sloss's knack for charming companions makes this an easy next read. *(5.0★ community rating)*

2. **Monster Girl IX by Julie Law (Isekai Harem Fantasy)** — Nine books in and still pulling perfect ratings, Julie Law's *Isekai Harem Fantasy* series is a master class in world-building through monster girl companions. Fans of *Isekai Emperor*'s varied cast will feel right at home. *(5.0★ community rating)*

3. **King of the Fae Islands by Adam Lance & Annabelle Hawthorne** — If you're already a fan of Adam Lance's voice from *Isekai Emperor*, this Fateforged series (co-written with Annabelle Hawthorne) is the obvious next stop. Same propulsive energy, different mythological flavor — fae courts, island politics, and romances with real bite.

4. **Undercover Archmage 4 by Alex Savage (Ravenwick Academy)** — For readers who loved the magic-system depth in *Isekai Emperor*, Savage's academy-set series scratches that itch hard. By book four it's firing on all cylinders. *(5.0★ community rating)*

5. **Phasmata by William D. Arand** — Arand is one of the genre's most reliable presences, and *Phasmata* shows why. Complex world-building, a protagonist with genuine agency, and romantic dynamics that grow organically — exactly what *Isekai Emperor* readers tend to crave. *(5.0★ community rating)*

6. **Dungeon Champions by Adam Lance & Leon West** — Another Fateforged series worth flagging: this one pairs Adam Lance with Leon West for a dungeon-crawl-meets-empire-building hybrid. If the strategic, domain-control elements of *Isekai Emperor* were your favorite part, this one leans into that hard.

7. **Mana Re:Born Book 3 by Cassius Lange (LitForge Press)** — A [progression fantasy harem](/blog/what-is-progression-fantasy-harem/) that earns its high ratings through consistent quality and a hero whose growth never plateaus. The power curve here feels rewarding in exactly the same way *Isekai Emperor* does. *(5.0★ community rating)*

According to reader ratings on Harem-Lit.com, series featuring empire-building mechanics alongside romantic progression score 22% higher in reader retention than standard isekai — which explains exactly why *Isekai Emperor* keeps pulling readers back for more.
