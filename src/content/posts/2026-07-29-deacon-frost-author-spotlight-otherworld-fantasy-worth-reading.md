[Academy harem fiction](/blog/what-is-academy-harem/) is a sub-genre of haremlit where the protagonist's journey of growth, bonding, and relationship-building unfolds within a structured learning environment. It is characterized by a sense of earned progress, a cast of distinct companions introduced through shared experience, and stakes that feel personal before they ever feel world-ending — and Deacon Frost writes it with real heart.

If you've been browsing the harem fantasy community looking for an author who commits fully to their world and their characters, Deacon Frost is a name you'll want to know. The Otherworld series — split across the completed Otherworld Academy run and the ongoing Otherworld Adventures — has built a quietly devoted readership, and for good reason.

## What Is the Otherworld Academy Series?

The Otherworld Academy series is Deacon Frost's flagship work: a completed harem fantasy following a protagonist named Levi through a richly built magical world. Like the best academy harem fiction, it earns its emotional payoffs by investing in the relationships first. By the time you reach the end of the main run, you've watched Levi grow into something genuinely worth rooting for — which makes the epilogue short story all the more satisfying.

## Should You Read *Nesting Day* Before or After the Main Series?

*Nesting Day: An Otherworld Academy Short Story* is designed to be read after the main Otherworld Academy series — it functions explicitly as an epilogue, and Deacon Frost frames it that way in the description itself. The story picks up with Levi following his honeymoon, heading to the Purple Peaks to make good on promises he made to a pair of ancient dragons named Maledict and Imri. If you know Levi, you already know those promises matter. If you don't, this is the kind of detail that makes you want to go back to the beginning immediately.

What's lovely about *Nesting Day* is that it does what the best epilogue fiction does: it gives readers closure while reminding them why they loved the journey in the first place. Ancient dragons being ancient about things — nothing is ever as easy as Levi hopes — is exactly the kind of warm, character-consistent conflict that rewards long-time readers. It currently holds a perfect 5.0 rating from community reviewers, which tells you fans felt the landing stuck.

Published in July 2024, *Nesting Day* is a natural celebration of everything the Otherworld Academy series built.

## Where Does *Otherworld Adventures* Fit In?

After wrapping the Academy arc, Deacon Frost launched Otherworld Adventures — and the series is already delivering. *Broken Trust*, the fourth entry in that series, published July 11, 2026, and carries a perfect 5.0 rating from early community reviews. The fact that a fourth installment is pulling that kind of response suggests this isn't a series running on fumes. Frost has clearly found something in the Otherworld setting that keeps expanding in interesting directions.

If you loved the Academy books and wondered where Levi's story could go next, Otherworld Adventures appears to be the answer — and *Broken Trust* landing at book four with those scores is a genuine signal that the serialized momentum is holding.

## Where to Start with Deacon Frost

If you're new to the author, here's a simple reading path the source material supports:

1. **Start with the Otherworld Academy series** — the main run, which the short story is an epilogue to.
2. **Read *Nesting Day*** as your epilogue and send-off from the Academy arc.
3. **Pick up Otherworld Adventures** if you want to stay in the world — and with four books in and strong ratings, there's real runway here.

## Who Else Is Writing in This Space?
