## Where Do Artists Find Romance and Harem Fantasy Cover Work?

Until now: scattered everywhere. Portfolio posts into the social void, general freelance sites where your fantasy romance work competes with corporate logo briefs, and author Discords you can only find if you already know somebody.

[Harem-Lit.com](https://harem-lit.com) — the community hub for HaremLit and men's romance — now runs a dedicated **cover artist application** into its vetted [Industry Professionals directory](https://harem-lit.com/industry-professionals). Editors, narrators, beta readers, and cover artists, all reviewed before their profiles go public, all searchable by the authors who need them.

Think about who's browsing that page. Not random shoppers — authors in *your* genre with a book in production. Many of them writing series, which is the magic word for a working artist. A HaremLit series means covers plural, plus character art the fans genuinely collect, audiobook covers, and ad creative. Land one series author and you've landed a pipeline, not a gig.

## How the Cover Artist Application Works

The application is free, takes 20–30 minutes the first time, auto-saves so you can finish it whenever, and gets human review within about 7 days. Here's what it's built around:

1. **A hosted gallery of 5–10 images.** Your originals stay private — the platform strips metadata and publishes only moderated display copies after approval. Your art, protected by default.
2. **Your styles, tiers, and genre comfort.** You declare what you make and what you charge for it — exact prices, ranges, or "ask me."
3. **Honest AI disclosure on every image** — human-made, AI-enhanced, or AI-generated, with detail required for anything not fully human. More on why this is your best friend in a second.
4. **A recorded rights attestation** that the work is yours to license.
5. **Author verification** — an author you've worked with can personally vouch for the collaboration. A portfolio with witnesses.
6. **Commercial licensing built in.** Every listed price must include a commercial publishing license. No "oh, commercial use is extra" surprises — for them or for you.

Approved artists get a real dashboard: profile views, unique visitors, contact reveals, and even the search terms authors used to find you. There's a pause button for when your queue fills up. And authors and publishers on paid plans can post **casting calls** — private paid briefs the platform matches to artists who fit, with your private rates and hard limits never shown to the poster.

## Why Human Artists Win Here

Let's say the quiet part warmly: this community *notices* cover art. Readers recognize an artist's style across series, follow them to new authors, and absolutely notice when a cover is generated slop. Authors know their audience feels that way.

That's why the disclosure system is the most artist-friendly thing on the platform. When every image carries an honest classification and a rights attestation, your "fully human-made" gallery isn't a marketing claim — it's a verified fact sitting right there on your profile. In a market drowning in generated images, *provable human craft is the premium product*, and the directory is built to display exactly that proof.

That's the bigger vision here, and it's one worth loving: the people who write these books, the people who read them, and the people who paint their worlds, all in one community, working together as humans to make the best books this genre has ever produced. Your art is a huge part of that.

## What Should Go in Your Portfolio?

Curating your 5–10 images for HaremLit authors? Show them:
